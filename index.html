Say it right the first time.

State bars police lawyer advertising. Best Lawyers hands you wording that already passes. Copy it, add your details, done.

Approved language

  • "Margaret Cole was included in the 2026 edition of The Best Lawyers in America® for Family Law."
  • "Caldwell & Pierce LLP is proud to have 14 lawyers recognized by Best Lawyers® in 2026 in America."
  • "Robert Brown was named the Best Lawyers® 2026 Family Law 'Lawyer of the Year' in Atlanta."

Not approved

  • "Richard Brown is a Best Lawyer."
  • "Richard Brown has been named a Texas Best Lawyer."
  • Any claim that recognition guarantees a result or endorses a product or service.
